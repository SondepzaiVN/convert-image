What is PNG?

PNG stores exact pixels with lossless compression and supports alpha transparency. It excels at flat-color graphics, but complex screenshots and photographic images may consume more bandwidth than necessary.

What is WebP?

WebP offers modern lossy and lossless image coding, including alpha transparency. It is widely supported in current browsers and is frequently used to optimize site assets.

When should I convert PNG to WebP?

  • You are optimizing PNG assets for a modern website.
  • A transparent image is too large and your target supports WebP.
  • You want adjustable quality for screenshots or mixed-content graphics.

Keep in mind: This converter exposes a quality control for WebP. At lower settings, fine text and hard edges may show artifacts; inspect important UI graphics at their actual display size.

Frequently asked questions

Does WebP keep transparency?

The WebP format supports alpha transparency, and the converter retains decoded alpha data.

Will every PNG become smaller?

No. Simple, already optimized PNG graphics can be competitive; always compare actual file sizes.

What quality should I use?

Start near the default and inspect the result. Text-heavy graphics often need a higher setting than photographs.

Are dimensions changed?

No. The tool converts the format without resizing the pixel dimensions.
