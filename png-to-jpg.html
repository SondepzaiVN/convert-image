What is PNG?

PNG is a lossless format that keeps sharp edges and can include transparent pixels. It works especially well for logos, screenshots, diagrams, and interface graphics, but photographic PNGs can be large.

What is JPG?

JPG compresses photographs efficiently by discarding some visual information. It cannot store transparency, but it is accepted almost everywhere and is usually compact for camera-style images.

When should I convert PNG to JPG?

  • A photographic PNG is too large for an upload or email.
  • A service accepts JPG but not PNG.
  • Transparency is not needed and broad compatibility matters.

Keep in mind: Transparent PNG pixels must be flattened because JPG has no alpha channel. This converter places them on a white background; do not use JPG when the transparent background must remain.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to a transparent background?

Transparent areas are placed on white because JPG cannot store transparency.

Can JPG be smaller than PNG?

Usually yes for photographs. For flat graphics or text, the result depends on the image and quality setting.

Is there a quality control?

Yes. Use the quality slider before conversion; a higher value usually means more detail and a larger file.

Can I process multiple PNG files?

Yes, up to 20 files per batch, with a 50 MB limit for each file.
