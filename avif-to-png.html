What is AVIF?

AVIF is a high-efficiency image format derived from AV1. It supports transparency and advanced color features while often producing small files, but decoding support varies in older tools.

What is PNG?

PNG is a broadly supported, lossless raster format. It handles alpha transparency reliably and is well suited to graphics and intermediate editing files.

When should I convert AVIF to PNG?

  • You need to edit an AVIF in software that supports PNG.
  • Transparency must remain available in the output.
  • A document or publishing workflow requires a lossless PNG file.

Keep in mind: PNG is likely to be much larger than AVIF and does not preserve every advanced AVIF color or HDR characteristic. It preserves the pixels produced by the browser-side decoder.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PNG output lossless?

PNG encoding is lossless for the decoded pixels, although the AVIF source may have used lossy compression.

Does transparency survive?

Yes, when transparency is present in the decoded source.

Why did the file size increase?

PNG trades compression efficiency for lossless, widely compatible storage.

Can I convert more than one AVIF?

Yes. The batch queue supports up to 20 files, each no larger than 50 MB.
