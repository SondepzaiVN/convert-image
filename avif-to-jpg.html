What is AVIF?

AVIF is a modern image format based on AV1 technology. It can deliver excellent compression and supports features such as transparency, but older apps and operating systems may not decode it.

What is JPG?

JPG is an established lossy format with exceptional software compatibility. It is straightforward to share and upload, especially for photographs without transparency.

When should I convert AVIF to JPG?

  • An older editor or viewer cannot open AVIF.
  • A form or publishing system only accepts JPG.
  • You need a simple, widely shareable photo file.

Keep in mind: JPG cannot retain AVIF transparency or HDR characteristics. Transparent areas are flattened, and output uses standard JPG encoding.

Frequently asked questions

Can JPG preserve AVIF transparency?

No. JPG has no alpha channel, so transparent pixels are flattened onto white.

Will HDR be preserved?

No. This utility produces a standard JPG and is not an HDR color-management workflow.

Why convert AVIF to JPG?

Compatibility is the main reason: JPG opens in more older applications and upload systems.

Does the app upload AVIF files?

No. The codecs run locally in a browser worker.
