What is WebP?

WebP is designed for efficient web delivery and can store lossy, lossless, or transparent images. Browser support is now broad, but some document tools and older desktop programs still reject it.

What is JPG?

JPG is a universally recognized lossy photo format. Its lack of transparency and older compression are trade-offs for excellent compatibility across software and services.

When should I convert WebP to JPG?

  • A desktop application cannot open a downloaded WebP image.
  • An upload field accepts JPG but rejects WebP.
  • You need a photo format that recipients can open without special software.

Keep in mind: Transparent WebP areas are flattened onto white in JPG. Re-encoding a lossy WebP as JPG may add compression artifacts, so use a high quality setting when possible.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to WebP transparency?

It is flattened onto a white background because JPG cannot represent transparent pixels.

Does converting improve quality?

No. Conversion changes compatibility, not the detail available in the source.

Can I choose JPG quality?

Yes. Set the quality slider before starting the conversion.

Are animations supported?

No. The current tool converts still images and does not preserve animated WebP frames.
