What is HEIC?

HEIC is an efficient image container often produced by Apple cameras. It saves storage space well, though support can be inconsistent outside newer Apple software and modern imaging tools.

What is PNG?

PNG stores images with lossless compression and supports an alpha channel for transparency. It is dependable for screenshots, graphics, interface assets, and images that will be edited repeatedly.

When should I convert HEIC to PNG?

  • You want lossless output for further editing.
  • Your destination supports PNG but cannot decode HEIC.
  • You need a web-friendly format that can represent transparency.

Keep in mind: PNG files are often much larger than HEIC photos. Converting to PNG preserves decoded pixels without another lossy step, but it cannot recover detail already absent from the source.

Frequently asked questions

Is HEIC to PNG lossless?

The PNG encoding is lossless, but the decoded HEIC source may itself have been encoded with loss.

Why is the PNG larger?

PNG prioritizes exact pixel storage rather than the photographic compression efficiency of HEIC.

Are HEIC files sent to a server?

No. Decoding and encoding take place locally in your browser.

Can I download a ZIP?
