What is WebP?

WebP is a compact web image format that supports both transparency and multiple compression modes. It is efficient in browsers, although compatibility can still be uneven in older creative and office software.

What is PNG?

PNG is a mature lossless format with reliable alpha transparency. It is a common interchange choice for logos, icons, screenshots, and other assets that need crisp edges.

When should I convert WebP to PNG?

  • A design or office application does not accept WebP.
  • You need lossless output for another round of editing.
  • The source has transparency that must be retained.

Keep in mind: PNG output can be considerably larger. Animated WebP is not preserved; the current converter is intended for still images only.

Frequently asked questions

Will transparency be preserved?

Yes, when the decoded WebP contains an alpha channel, PNG can store it.

Why is the PNG much larger?

PNG uses general lossless compression, while WebP may use more space-efficient lossy coding.

Does it convert animated WebP?

No. This tool is for still images and does not preserve animation.

Is processing private?

Yes. Files are decoded and encoded on your device and are not uploaded by the application.
